FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Talk to our team →

Who can join ANSTA?
GNM, B.Sc. Nursing and Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing graduates, experienced nurses seeking upskilling, and eligible candidates for ANM training (10th pass).
Is clinical training included?
Yes. All eligible programs include a supervised clinical internship across real hospital departments.
Do you provide placement assistance?
Yes. We support students with career guidance, interview preparation, soft-skills training and hospital placement assistance (subject to vacancies and employer requirements).
Do I receive a certificate?
Yes. On successful completion you receive an ANSTA Course Completion Certificate, a Clinical Internship Certificate and a Skill Competency Assessment Report.
