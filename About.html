About ANSTA

Competent, confident, compassionate nurses.

ANSTA — Advanced Nursing Skill Training Academy — is a dedicated clinical training institute. We bridge the gap between classroom education and real-world hospital practice through structured training, simulation, and supervised clinical exposure.

Vision

To become India's most trusted nursing skill development academy — producing highly competent professionals who deliver safe, ethical, quality patient care.

Mission
→Bridge the gap between nursing education and clinical practice. →Enhance practical competency through hands-on learning. →Build confidence for independent clinical practice. →Promote patient safety and quality healthcare. →Prepare nurses for successful hospital careers.

Why ANSTA was started

Many nursing graduates possess strong theoretical knowledge but have limited confidence applying clinical skills in real healthcare settings. ANSTA was established to provide structured practical training, simulation-based learning, and supervised hospital exposure — helping graduates transition smoothly into professional practice.
